The Pool

CRUS11TOUR is a golf survivor (one-and-done) pool on Splash Sports. Pick one golfer per tournament. Points equal their dollar earnings. Once you use a player, they're gone for the season. 40 players available across 30 events — resource management is everything.

Event Tiers

Events are tiered by purse size and prestige. Since points = earnings, higher-purse events offer more upside for your best players.

TierEventsPurse RangeStrategy
Tier 1Majors, Signature, Players, FedEx$17M — $100MUse your best available players here
Tier 2Above-average purse events$8M — $9.1MGood value picks — strong players at lower risk
Tier 3Standard tour events$4M — $8.7MUse mid-ranked players, save elites

The key tradeoff: using Scheffler at a $7.5M event "works" but wastes his upside. Save him for a Major ($20M+) where a win earns 3x as much.

Player Scoring

Rankings: Data Golf

We use Data Golf rankings instead of the Official World Golf Rankings (OWGR). Why? OWGR penalizes LIV Golf players who play fewer sanctioned events, making them look artificially weak. At Majors, where LIV players compete, OWGR rankings would underestimate players like Rahm, Koepka, or DeChambeau. Data Golf's model is based on actual scoring ability, making it fairer across tours.

The composite score

Each available player gets scored on five factors that combine into a single number:

Score = (Value x 3) + Course Fit + Form + Injury

Value score

How "underpriced" a player is by the betting market.

Value = DG Rank / Odds Position

If a player is ranked #5 in the world but only the 10th betting favorite (odds position = 10), their value score is 5/10 = 0.5. If they're ranked #5 and the 3rd favorite, value = 5/3 = 1.67. Higher value = the market is sleeping on them relative to their actual skill. This gets triple-weighted because finding undervalued picks is the biggest edge in a survivor pool.

Injury penalty

Withdrawals are the worst outcome in survivor — you burn a player and get zero points. The penalty is harsh by design:

0
Healthy — no concerns
-2
Minor concern — monitoring
-10
Significant withdrawal risk — avoid
